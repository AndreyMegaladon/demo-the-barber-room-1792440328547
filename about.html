Meie Lugu

A gentleman's barber nestled in the historic Old Town of Tallinn at Lai 44. Located in a notorious 1930's building, we have created a space where you can come, relax and enjoy a clean haircut and a sharp beard trim. Our skilled barbers specialise in a wide range of services, ensuring every visit leaves you feeling refreshed and looking your best. Step into our friendly and welcoming atmosphere for an experience that goes beyond just a haircut, where community and quality come together.
